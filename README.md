# Gippitify
*Make the internet worse by turning any text into AI slop.*

## Why make this project?
Why not? ¯\\_(ツ)\_/¯

In all seriousness, most people often don't recognise heavily AI generated or modified text. By showing people blatantly "slop" text, it might help people recognise the trademark warning signs of AI content.
